Storage Beds With Drawers, Handmade in Britain

A storage bed with drawers is one of the simplest ways to add practical space to a bedroom without changing how the room feels. Rather than a lift-up ottoman base, drawers built into the frame slide open from the side, so you can access spare bedding, clothing or everyday items without needing to move the mattress.

Our collection offers a choice of two-drawer and four-drawer configurations, so you can pick the amount of storage that suits your bedroom, from a compact guest room to a busy family bedroom. Every frame is handmade to order on a solid, supportive base, finished with the same tailored upholstery as the rest of our range.

Available across the full range of UK bed sizes, each storage drawer bed comes with our standard 24-month warranty and easy returns, so you can add practical storage to your bedroom with confidence.

FAQs

Storage Drawer Bed Questions Answered

Our storage drawer beds are available with either two or four drawers built into the base, depending on the design. The exact drawer count for each bed is shown on its product page.

An Ottoman bed uses a gas-lift base that lifts the whole mattress to reveal storage underneath. A storage drawer bed instead has drawers built into the sides of the frame that slide open independently, so you can access storage without disturbing the mattress or bedding.

Every storage drawer bed is available in Single, Small Double, Double, King and Super King. Exact frame dimensions for each size are shown on the product's detail page.

Every storage drawer bed comes with our standard 24-month warranty, plus 30-day easy returns on unused, unassembled beds.
