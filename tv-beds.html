TV Beds, Handmade in Britain

A TV bed brings your television and your bed frame together in one considered piece of furniture, using a built-in lift mechanism hidden inside the footboard to raise the screen to the perfect viewing height and lower it flush out of sight when you're ready to sleep. It's a smart way to add a television to a bedroom without needing a separate stand, unit, or wall bracket.

Every TV bed in our collection is handmade to order on a solid, supportive frame, finished with the same tailored upholstery as the rest of our range. The lift mechanism itself is smooth and quiet, operated by a simple wireless remote, with cable routing built into the frame to keep wiring tidy and out of view.

Available across a range of UK sizes and screen-size compatibility, every TV bed comes with our standard 24-month warranty and easy returns, so you can add this piece of considered, practical design to your bedroom with confidence.
