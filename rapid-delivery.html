Rapid Delivery Beds Made for Modern Bedrooms

Our rapid delivery beds are selected specifically for faster turnaround, so you don't have to wait months for a beautifully made bed frame. Each one is handmade in Britain to the same standard as the rest of our range, with selected sizes and fabrics available for quicker dispatch — ideal when you need a new bed sooner rather than later.

Upholstered beds bring a soft, tailored finish to the bedroom and suit most modern interiors, while ottoman beds add gas-lift storage beneath the mattress, making the most of the space without needing extra furniture. Storage beds take a similar practical approach with integrated drawers, well suited to smaller bedrooms where every bit of space counts.

Getting the size right matters as much as the style: a Single or Small Double suits a compact room or a single sleeper, a Double gives one person generous space, and a King or Super King gives couples enough room to sleep comfortably without disturbing each other. Our bed sizes and dimensions table above shows the exact frame measurements for each size, so you can check it fits your room before you order.

Choosing a fabric and colour is largely down to personal taste and the rest of your room's palette — plush and velvet finishes bring a softer, more luxurious feel, while linen, bouclé and chenille offer a more textured, relaxed look. Whichever you choose, every rapid delivery bed comes with our standard 24-month warranty and easy returns, so you can order with confidence.

We're Here to Help

Need Help Choosing Your Bed?

It depends on your room and who's sleeping in it. A Single or Small Double suits a compact room, a Double gives a single sleeper plenty of space, and couples usually find a King or Super King noticeably more comfortable. Check the dimensions table above against your room's measurements before deciding.

An ottoman bed is a great option if you're short on storage space. The gas-lift base lifts the entire mattress to reveal a large storage area beneath, ideal for bedding, luggage or seasonal items you don't need every day.

Plush and velvet fabrics feel soft and luxurious and suit a more formal bedroom. Linen, bouclé and chenille bring a relaxed, textured look that works well in a modern or minimal room. Choose whichever best matches the rest of your bedroom's palette and feel.
