Luxury Beds Handmade for Exceptional Comfort and Style

Every bed we build starts with a simple idea: a bedroom should be the most comfortable room in the house, not an afterthought. Our frames are handcrafted in Britain by upholsterers who take the time to get the details right, from the density of the padding to the line of a stitched seam, so the finished piece feels as good as it looks.

We work with quality timber frames, supportive sprung slats and a curated range of premium fabrics, choosing materials for how they wear over years, not just how they photograph on day one. The result is a bed that holds its shape, keeps its comfort and still looks considered long after the box has been recycled.

Storage is built in where it earns its place, not bolted on as an afterthought. Ottoman lift-ups, drawer bases and TV beds all use the same tailored upholstery as our standard frames, so a practical bedroom never has to mean a compromised one. Every style is designed to sit comfortably in a modern bedroom, whether that's a compact city flat or a spacious main suite.

Designed for Comfort and Convenience

A well-designed bed earns its place in the room every single night, so comfort always comes first. We pair supportive frames with considered proportions, thoughtful headboard heights and finishes that feel as good under your hand as they look across the room, so the bed works as hard as the rest of your furniture.

Practical features are woven in rather than added on: soft-close ottoman mechanisms, generously sized drawers and cable-tidy TV lifts all sit quietly behind the same tailored upholstery, so nothing about the design has to feel like a trade-off between form and function.

Customer Support

Choosing a new bed is a big decision, so our team is on hand to help with fabric and size choices, storage options, delivery timings or any other product questions before or after you order.
