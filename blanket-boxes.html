Blanket Boxes for Stylish Bedroom Storage

A well-chosen blanket box does more than tidy away spare bedding — it brings a considered, finished feel to a bedroom while quietly solving a very practical problem. Duvets, throws and out-of-season linens all need somewhere to live, and an ottoman-style storage box at the foot of the bed keeps them close at hand without cluttering wardrobes or shelves.

Our collection covers a range of fabric finishes, from soft plush and tactile bouclé to tailored linen-look weaves and rich velvet, so you can match the tone of your existing bedroom furniture rather than working around it. Many of our boxes also double as extra seating, with a padded, upholstered lid that's comfortable enough for pulling on shoes or laying out tomorrow's outfit.

Every blanket box in the range is built on a solid frame designed for daily use in a UK home, with construction quality that holds up over years rather than seasons. Whether you're organising a compact city bedroom or finishing a spacious main suite, a blanket box is a simple way to add storage and style in one piece of furniture.
