Solid Ottoman Beds, Handmade in Britain

A Solid Ottoman Bed pairs a reinforced solid base with a spacious gas-lift storage compartment beneath the mattress, offering a sturdier alternative to a slatted base while keeping all the practical storage benefits of an ottoman design.

Every bed in this collection is handmade to order, with the gas-lift mechanism designed for smooth, everyday use even with a fully dressed mattress. Underneath, the storage compartment comfortably holds spare bedding, out-of-season clothing or anything else you'd rather keep out of sight.

Available across the full range of UK bed sizes from Single 3ft through to Super King 6ft, every Solid Ottoman Bed comes with our standard 24-month warranty and easy returns.
