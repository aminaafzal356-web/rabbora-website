Premium Mattresses for Better Sleep

Choosing the right mattress starts with understanding how you sleep. Memory foam mattresses slowly contour to the body, easing pressure at the hips and shoulders and reducing motion transfer between sleeping partners — a good fit for side sleepers or anyone sharing a bed. Pocket spring mattresses use individually wrapped coils that move independently, giving even support across the whole sleep surface while still feeling responsive underfoot.

Hybrid mattresses combine both approaches, layering foam comfort over a pocket spring base for a balance of contouring softness and structured support. Orthopaedic mattresses take a firmer, more structured approach, designed to keep the spine aligned through the night — often recommended for those who prefer a supportive, stable sleep surface or experience back discomfort.

Firmness is a personal preference as much as a practical one: softer mattresses suit side sleepers who need more cushioning at the hips and shoulders, while firmer options generally suit back and front sleepers who need more even support. Getting the size right matters too — a Double gives a single sleeper generous space, while couples often find a King or Super King noticeably more comfortable for uninterrupted sleep.

Whichever construction you choose, look for durable materials and a mattress built to hold its support over years of everyday use, not just its first few nights. Every mattress in our collection is designed with that longevity in mind, backed by our standard warranty and fast UK delivery.

Need Help Choosing Your Mattress?

It depends how you sleep. Side sleepers usually feel more comfortable on a softer mattress that cushions the hips and shoulders. Back and front sleepers tend to prefer a medium or firm feel that keeps the spine supported. If you're unsure, a medium mattress is a safe, versatile starting point.

A Single or Small Double works well for one person or a smaller room. A Double gives a single sleeper plenty of space, while couples usually find a King or Super King noticeably more comfortable, with enough room to move without disturbing each other.

Memory foam slowly contours to the body for a close, cushioned feel. Hybrid mattresses layer foam over pocket springs, combining that contouring comfort with the responsive, breathable support springs provide — a good middle ground if you want softness without losing structure.
