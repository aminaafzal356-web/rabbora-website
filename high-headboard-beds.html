High Headboard Beds, Handmade in Britain

A high headboard bed brings a naturally striking focal point to a bedroom, with the headboard itself doing most of the visual work above a supportive, comfortable frame. Ours range from 130cm to 150cm tall, each finished with deep-buttoned upholstered detailing for a tailored, characterful silhouette.

Every bed in this collection is handmade to order on a solid, supportive base with a sprung slatted top for comfortable, breathable sleep, using the same premium fabrics and construction standards as the rest of our range.

Available across the full range of UK bed sizes, every High Headboard Bed comes with our standard 24-month warranty and easy returns, so you can bring real presence to your bedroom with confidence.

High Headboard Bed Questions Answered

Headboard height varies by design, from 130cm to 150cm tall. The exact height for each bed is shown on its product page.
