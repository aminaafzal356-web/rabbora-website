Slatted Ottoman Beds

A Slatted Ottoman Bed pairs a reinforced slatted base with a spacious gas-lift storage compartment beneath the mattress, making it one of the most practical ways to add a bed and useful storage to a room without needing extra furniture. The slatted base supports the mattress evenly while allowing airflow underneath, which helps keep it fresher for longer than a solid base.

Every bed in this collection is handmade to order, with the gas-lift mechanism designed for smooth, everyday lifting even when the mattress is fully dressed. Underneath, the storage compartment comfortably holds spare bedding, out-of-season clothing or anything else you'd rather keep out of sight — a genuinely useful way to optimise space in a smaller bedroom.

Comfort and durability come from a solid handmade frame paired with a tailored upholstered finish, available across the full range of UK bed sizes from Single 3ft through to Super King 6ft. Fabric choices span plush, velvet, linen, bouclé and more, so you can match the bed to your existing bedroom palette rather than working around it.
