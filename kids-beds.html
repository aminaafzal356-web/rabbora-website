Kids’ Beds, Handmade in Britain

Choosing a first proper bed for a child means balancing comfort and character with practical safety. Our Kids' Beds are built with a deliberately low, stable profile and rounded, durable construction, so they feel as safe as they look, while still bringing the same handmade quality as the rest of our range.

Every frame is finished in an easy-to-clean, tailored upholstery designed to withstand everyday life in a child's room, and built on a solid, supportive base with a sprung slatted top for comfortable, breathable sleep.

Available in Single and Small Double sizes to suit a growing room, every Kids' Bed comes with our standard 24-month warranty and easy returns, so you can furnish a child's bedroom with confidence.
